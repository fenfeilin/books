1. 内容介绍
	Ducky和Gyuri 要出去玩，Gyuri很贪心，想带上球，水枪，捕虫罐，捕蝶网，钓鱼竿，绳子，水桶，铲子，手电筒，相机。Ducky实在没办法，只能拿出小车。Gyuri觉得小车太小，结果Ducky一变，成了一个好大的车。Gyuri推车的时候，终于觉得自己带了太多东西。
	
2. 重点单词 & 句型
	- 重点单词：ball(s), water gun(s), carry, bug jar(s), butterfly net(s), fishing rod(s), rope(s), bucket(s), shovel(s), flashlight(s), camera(s), wagon(s)
	- 句型: 
		- can i take my ...?
		- that's a lot of stuff?
		- how can you carry it?
	
3. 拓展
	（1）根据封面猜一猜：
	- What is the duck going to do with the basket ?
	（2）看内容可以互动：
	- toy car，badminton racket，inflatable wagon
	（3）看完的理解：
	- who：Ducky、Gyuri
	- what：
		- Going on a picnic
		- they have lot of things to bring
