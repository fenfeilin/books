1. 内容介绍
	bella、tenny 和 Gyuri去动物园，看到了长颈鹿，大猩猩，北极熊，鳄鱼，老虎。Gyuri想把他们带回家，但是遭到bella 和 tenny的拒绝。正当Gyuri垂头丧气的时候，bella、tenny带着Gyuri走到了玩具店，Gyuri开心的抱着“老虎”回家啦。
2. 重点单词 & 句型
	- 重点单词：giraffe(s), gorilla(s), alligator(s), tiger(s), polar bear(s)
	- 句型: 
		- what's that? it's a/an
		- can i have it?
		- no way. yes you can
3. 拓展
	（1）根据封面猜一猜：
	- Where are they going? -- zoo
	（2）看内容可以互动：
	- The giraffe is too tall, and the house is too short to fit in.
	- Gorillas can destroy things at home.
	- The place where polar bears live is too cold.
	- A crocodile needs to live in a pond, not in a bathtub.
	- The tiger snores while sleeping and also scratches the wall.
	（3）看完的理解：
	- who：bella、tenny 和 Gyuri
	- where：zoo
	- why：Why can't Gyuri bring animals home?
	- what：What did Gyuri bring home?
