## 一、这套书总体讲什么（主题 · 难度 · 怎么读）

### 📖 总体讲什么

**Buddy Readers E&F 级**是一套「故事 + 概念认知 + 思维启蒙」三合一的早期分级读物。最大特色是**用拟人化的水果好朋友 Apple 和 Orange 串起整套书**——孩子在反复见到同样的角色中产生熟悉感，愿意一本接一本读下去。和 B 级的「纯句型替换」不同，E&F 级开始有**完整情节、转折和结尾提问**，是从「会跟读」走向「能理解、爱思考」的关键一跃。

16 本可分成 **4 大主题板块**：

| 板块 | 册数 | 包含书目 | 教学价值 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🍎 **友谊成长**（Apple & Orange 系列） | 7 | 01 Meet / 02 Birthday / 03 Costume Party / 04 The Great Race / 11 The Bake / 12 Rainy Day / 13 Hat Mystery | 社交情感（SEL）：交朋友、分享、互助、接纳差异 |
| 🌍 **概念认知 / 科普** | 4 | 06 Best Pet / 08 My Earth Friend / 09 Over the Rainbow / 14 Guess Who | 动物、颜色、环保、分类与描述 |
| 🦄 **想象创意** | 3 | 05 Planet Zog / 07 Cloud Pictures / 10 Unicorns Dance | 发散思维、比喻、画面想象 |
| 🔍 **推理悬疑**（思维启蒙） | 2 | 15 Missing Snowman / 16 Who Took the Bone | 找线索、推因果、逻辑启蒙 |

### 🎯 主题关键词
`友谊` `分享互助` `生日派对` `化装扮演` `比赛` `宠物` `颜色彩虹` `环保` `云朵想象` `外星球` `动物猜谜` `烘焙` `方位介词` `推理找线索`

### 📊 难度定位

处于**「自然拼读巩固期 → 早期独立阅读」**的过渡阶段：

| 维度 | 具体表现 |
|---|---|
| 句长 | 多为 3–7 词的短句（*"A dog can run and play."*） |
| 语法 | 一般现在时为主，穿插一般过去时（*was / made / ran*）；出现引语 *"...," said Apple.* |
| 句型 | **高度重复、可预测**，同一本反复套用一个句型，是早期阅读理想的「句型脚手架」 |
| 词汇 | 高频词（sight words）为主，穿插主题词：*costume, superhero, recycle, trunk, squirrel, puddle, twirl* |
| 互动 | 多本以提问收尾（*"What do you see in the clouds?"*），天然适合亲子对话 |
| 对标 | 约 RAZ aa–C / 牛津树 1–2 阶 / Lexile BR–150L / CEFR Pre-A1～A1 |
| 进阶顺序 | [[Buddy Reader B]]（纯句型替换）→ **E&F 级（有情节+提问+推理）** |

> 一句话总结：**「听得懂、能跟读、句型猜得出下一句，还开始爱想为什么」**——积累「我能读英文」自信的黄金阶段。

### 👶 适合怎么读
- **适龄**：4–6 岁、有一定听力基础、启蒙约 1 年、准备从「亲子听读」走向「自主认读」的孩子。
- **节奏**：一本重复 3–5 遍胜过 5 本各读一遍；按主题板块推进，读熟一个板块再换。
- **黄金流程**：**先听 → 再指读 → 后跟读 → 最后聊一聊**。

## 二、详细带读指导（备课三阶段）

借鉴 [[古力小超人]] 的三阶段备课法，结合 E&F 级「有情节、有提问」的特点落地：

### 1️⃣ 第一阶段：词汇拓展期（听得懂）
- 先**放音频/指读纯磨耳朵**，不强求开口。读到名词就**指着图上对应的东西**，建立「音—图」对应。
- 提前查好每本的主题词（见下方分册「核心词汇」），读前用封面玩 **Picture Walk**：「你看这是谁？猜猜会发生什么？」激活好奇。
- 目标：听到 *trunk* 能指对大象鼻子，听到 *twirl* 能转个圈。

### 2️⃣ 第二阶段：句型拓展期（开得了口）
- **留白接读**：读到固定句型的最后一个词停下，用眼神/手势邀请孩子接龙。
  - 例：*"A ___ is a good pet."* → 孩子补 *"dog!"*
- **替换造句**：句型不变换名词。*"That cloud looks like a ___"* → 让孩子自由填。
- **角色配音**：对话句（*"...," said Apple.*）用不同声音演 Apple / Orange，孩子会笑、会模仿。
- 用 **二选一提问** 降门槛：*"Is it red or blue?"*

### 3️⃣ 第三阶段：生活应用期（用得出来）
- 把内容搬进真实场景，脱离书本自然输出：
  - 读完《The Bake》真的烤一次饼干，边做边说 *flour / mix / oven*。
  - 读完《Cloud Pictures》带孩子抬头看云：*"That cloud looks like a ___?"*
  - 读完《My Earth Friend》今天做一件环保小事（关水龙头 / 垃圾分类）。
- 关键：**同一句型，书里读一遍，生活里用一遍**，记得又快又牢。

### 🔑 E&F 级专属带读技巧
- **善用结尾提问**：每本结尾的问句先英文问、再中文兜底，鼓励孩子用书里句型回答，蹦一个词也大力表扬。
- **推理本要「停下来问」**：读《Missing Snowman》《Who Took the Bone》时，每出现一条线索就停下问「你发现了什么？凶手是谁？」，最后揭晓时一起体会「原来如此」的快感。
- **方位介词配游戏**：读《The Hat Mystery》后玩「找东西」，用 under / in / behind 说位置。
- **动作词配 TPR**：《Unicorns Dance》的 twirl / leap / spin、《Guess Who》的 hop，都边读边做。

## 三、分册详细内容 + 熏听文本

> 每本标注：内容简介 · 核心词汇 · 核心句型 · 场景互动；点开 `<details>` 可看完整熏听文本（依音频整理）。

### 🍎 板块一：Apple & Orange 友谊成长系列

#### 01. Meet Apple and Orange（认识好朋友 · 异同对比）
- **内容简介**：Apple 和 Orange 是好朋友，一起玩、一起读书、一起吃零食，但他们也有很多不一样——颜色、形状、爱好都不同。不一样，也能是最好的朋友。
- **核心词汇**：best friends、different、red、orange、round、long、run、jump。
- **核心句型**：`We do everything together.` / `I am ___ . / I like to ___.` / `We are different, but we are best friends.`
- **场景互动**：和孩子说一个「我和好朋友不一样」的地方，体会差异也很美好。
<details>
<summary>🎵 点击展开熏听文本</summary>
Hello, I'm Apple. And I'm Orange. We are best friends. <br>
We do everything together. We play games. We read books. We eat snacks. <br>
But we are different. I am red. I am orange. I am round. I am long. <br>
I like to run. I like to jump. <br>
We are different, but we are best friends. The end.
</details>

#### 02. Apple's Awesome Birthday（生日惊喜 · 过去式动作）
- **内容简介**：Apple 生日到了，Orange 偷偷准备惊喜——做蛋糕、吹气球、挂横幅。"Surprise!" Apple 太开心了，两个朋友一起吃蛋糕跳舞。
- **核心词汇**：birthday、surprise、cake、balloons、banner、happy。
- **核心句型**：`Orange made a ___ . / Orange blew up ___ .`（过去式动词链）/ `This is the best birthday!`
- **场景互动**：画一张生日卡片，对家人说 *Happy Birthday!*
<details>
<summary>🎵 点击展开熏听文本</summary>
It was Apple's birthday. Orange had a plan. "I will make a surprise!" said Orange. <br>
Orange made a cake. Orange blew up balloons. Orange hung a banner. <br>
"Surprise!" Orange shouted. Apple was so happy. "This is the best birthday!" said Apple. <br>
The two friends ate cake. They danced and played. It was an awesome birthday. The end.
</details>

#### 03. The Costume Party（化装舞会 · 扮演）
- **内容简介**：化装舞会到了，Apple 想当超级英雄，Orange 想当海盗。穿上披风和帽子，和朋友们跳舞、玩游戏、吃美食，这是最棒的派对！
- **核心词汇**：costume、party、superhero、pirate、cape、hat。
- **核心句型**：`Apple wanted to be a ___ . / Apple put on a ___ .` / `It was the best party ever.`
- **场景互动**：用家里的围巾/帽子 cosplay 一个角色，问 *"Who are you?"*
<details>
<summary>🎵 点击展开熏听文本</summary>
It was the day of the costume party. Apple wanted to be a superhero. Orange wanted to be a pirate. <br>
Apple put on a cape. Orange put on a hat. "We look great!" they said. <br>
They went to the party. All their friends were there. <br>
They danced and played games. They ate yummy food. It was the best party ever. The end.
</details>

#### 04. The Great Race（友谊赛跑 · 比较级 + 价值观）
- **内容简介**：大赛跑开始！Apple 跑得快，Orange 跑得更快，但 Orange 摔倒了。Apple 停下来扶他，两人一起到终点。没赢，却很开心——做个好朋友才是最好的奖品。
- **核心词汇**：race、win、fast/faster、fell down、help、prize。
- **核心句型**：`Apple ran fast. Orange ran faster.` / `"Are you okay?"` / `Being a good friend is the best prize.`
- **场景互动**：聊聊「朋友摔倒了你会怎么做」，引导「友谊比输赢更重要」。
<details>
<summary>🎵 点击展开熏听文本</summary>
It was the day of the great race. Apple and Orange wanted to win. "Ready, set, go!" <br>
Apple ran fast. Orange ran faster. But then Orange fell down. <br>
Apple stopped to help. "Are you okay?" asked Apple. "Yes, thank you," said Orange. <br>
They finished the race together. They did not win, but they were happy. <br>
Being a good friend is the best prize. The end.
</details>

#### 11. The Bake（一起烤饼干 · 步骤顺序）
- **内容简介**：Apple 和 Orange 一起烤饼干——拿面粉、糖、鸡蛋，搅拌，放进烤箱。香喷喷的饼干出炉，"Yum!" 烘焙真有趣。
- **核心词汇**：bake、cookies、flour、sugar、eggs、mix、oven。
- **核心句型**：`They got the ___ . / They mixed it all together.`（first→then 顺序）/ `"Yum!"`
- **场景互动**：真的一起做一次饼干，边做边说步骤词。
<details>
<summary>🎵 点击展开熏听文本</summary>
Apple and Orange wanted to bake. "Let's make cookies!" said Apple. <br>
They got the flour. They got the sugar. They got the eggs. They mixed it all together. <br>
They put the cookies in the oven. Soon the cookies were ready. They smelled so good. <br>
Apple and Orange ate the cookies. "Yum!" they said. Baking is fun. The end.
</details>

#### 12. The Rainy Day（雨天搭城堡 · 提议句型）
- **内容简介**：下雨天不能出去玩，怎么办？Orange 提议搭城堡！用毯子和枕头搭了个大堡垒，在里面读书、讲故事。雨天也很有趣。
- **核心词汇**：rainy、fort、blankets、pillows、stories。
- **核心句型**：`"What can we do?"` / `"Let's ___!"`（提议句型）
- **场景互动**：用毯子和枕头搭一个 fort，钻进去读这本书。
<details>
<summary>🎵 点击展开熏听文本</summary>
It was a rainy day. Apple and Orange could not play outside. "What can we do?" asked Apple. <br>
"Let's build a fort!" said Orange. They got blankets. They got pillows. They built a big fort. <br>
They read books in the fort. They told stories in the fort. <br>
The rainy day was fun after all. The end.
</details>

#### 13. The Hat Mystery（帽子去哪了 · 方位介词）
- **内容简介**：Apple 的帽子不见了！床底下、衣柜里、门后面……到处找。"是不是在你头上？" Apple 一摸——帽子一直都在头上！两人哈哈大笑。
- **核心词汇**：lost、hat、under、in、behind、head。
- **核心句型**：`They looked under / in / behind ___ .`（方位介词集中训练）/ `It was there all along!`
- **场景互动**：玩「找东西」游戏，用 under / in / behind 说位置。
<details>
<summary>🎵 点击展开熏听文本</summary>
Apple lost his hat. "Where is my hat?" asked Apple. Orange helped Apple look. <br>
They looked under the bed. They looked in the closet. They looked behind the door. <br>
"Is it on your head?" asked Orange. Apple touched his head. <br>
The hat was there all along! They both laughed. The end.
</details>

### 🌍 板块二：概念认知 / 科普系列

#### 06. The Best Pet（最好的宠物 · 表达观点）
- **内容简介**：什么是最好的宠物？狗会跑会玩、猫又软又暖、鱼会游、鸟会唱……每种都很好。对你来说，最好的宠物是什么？
- **核心词汇**：pet、dog、cat、fish、bird、soft、swim、sing。
- **核心句型**：`A ___ is a good pet. A ___ can ___ .`（高复现，最佳接龙句）/ `What is the best pet for you?`
- **场景互动**：全家投票选「最想养的宠物」，让孩子说理由。
<details>
<summary>🎵 点击展开熏听文本</summary>
What is the best pet? A dog is a good pet. A dog can run and play. <br>
A cat is a good pet. A cat is soft and warm. <br>
A fish is a good pet. A fish can swim. <br>
A bird is a good pet. A bird can sing. <br>
What is the best pet for you? The end.
</details>

#### 08. My Earth Friend（地球好朋友 · 环保）
- **内容简介**：地球给我们空气、水、食物，我们也要爱护它——种树、节约用水、捡垃圾、回收。做地球的好朋友吧！
- **核心词汇**：Earth、air、water、food、plant trees、save water、trash、recycle。
- **核心句型**：`The Earth gives us ___ . / We can ___ .`
- **场景互动**：今天做一件环保小事（关水龙头 / 垃圾分类）。
<details>
<summary>🎵 点击展开熏听文本</summary>
I love the Earth. The Earth gives us air. The Earth gives us water. The Earth gives us food. <br>
We must take care of the Earth. We can plant trees. We can save water. <br>
We can pick up trash. We can recycle. Let's be a friend to the Earth. The end.
</details>

#### 09. Over the Rainbow（彩虹的颜色 · 颜色 + 比喻）
- **内容简介**：雨后出现彩虹，红橙黄绿蓝紫六种颜色——红得像苹果、黄得像太阳、绿得像草地……彩虹真美。
- **核心词汇**：rainbow、red、orange、yellow、green、blue、purple。
- **核心句型**：`Red like an apple. Yellow like the sun.`（颜色 + like 比喻）
- **场景互动**：在家找出 6 种颜色的东西排成小彩虹；问「还有什么是红色的？」
<details>
<summary>🎵 点击展开熏听文本</summary>
After the rain, I see a rainbow. A rainbow has many colors. <br>
Red, orange, yellow, green, blue, and purple. <br>
Red like an apple. Orange like an orange. Yellow like the sun. <br>
Green like the grass. Blue like the sky. Purple like a grape. <br>
Rainbows are beautiful. The end.
</details>

#### 14. Guess Who（动物猜猜猜 · 描述特征）
- **内容简介**：我在想一种动物——它很大、灰色、有长鼻子、大耳朵，爱吃花生。猜到了吗？是大象！再来一题：小小的、会跳、长耳朵、爱吃胡萝卜——是兔子！
- **核心词汇**：elephant、rabbit、big、gray、trunk、ears、hop、peanuts、carrots。
- **核心句型**：`It is ___ . It has a ___ . Can you guess who?`（描述 + 猜谜）
- **场景互动**：家长和孩子互相出动物谜语，轮流描述特征。
<details>
<summary>🎵 点击展开熏听文本</summary>
I am thinking of an animal. It is big. It is gray. It has a long trunk. <br>
It has big ears. It likes to eat peanuts. Can you guess who? It is an elephant! <br>
Now you try. I am thinking of an animal. It is small. It can hop. <br>
It has long ears. It likes carrots. Can you guess who? It is a rabbit! The end.
</details>

### 🦄 板块三：想象创意系列

#### 05. Life on Planet Zog（外星球生活 · 描述反差）
- **内容简介**：欢迎来到 Zog 星球！这里好奇怪——树是蓝的、草是粉的、外星人有三只眼、单脚跳、吃紫色食物、说 "Bleep bloop!"，还养着黏糊糊的宠物。你想去吗？
- **核心词汇**：planet、alien、strange、three eyes、hop、blob、visit。
- **核心句型**：`The trees are blue. The grass is pink.`（名词 + 颜色反差）/ `Would you like to visit?`
- **场景互动**：画一个你自己的外星人和外星球，描述它有什么不一样。
<details>
<summary>🎵 点击展开熏听文本</summary>
Welcome to Planet Zog. On Planet Zog, things are strange. <br>
The trees are blue. The grass is pink. The aliens have three eyes. They hop on one leg. <br>
They eat purple food. They speak a funny language. "Bleep bloop!" they say. <br>
They have a pet. It is a slimy blob. Life on Planet Zog is fun. Would you like to visit? The end.
</details>

#### 07. Cloud Pictures（云朵变变变 · 比喻想象）
- **内容简介**：我喜欢看云，云可以变成好多东西——像绵羊、像龙、像小船、像冰淇淋蛋筒。你在云里看到了什么？
- **核心词汇**：clouds、sheep、dragon、boat、ice cream cone、see。
- **核心句型**：`That cloud looks like a ___ .`（look like 比喻）/ `What do you see in the clouds?`
- **场景互动**：真的去户外看云，玩「云朵像什么」自由想象。
<details>
<summary>🎵 点击展开熏听文本</summary>
I like to look at clouds. Clouds can look like many things. <br>
That cloud looks like a sheep. That cloud looks like a dragon. <br>
That cloud looks like a boat. That cloud looks like an ice cream cone. <br>
What do you see in the clouds? The end.
</details>

#### 10. Unicorns Dance（独角兽跳舞 · 动作词）
- **内容简介**：从前有一群爱跳舞的独角兽，早上、晚上、雨里、阳光下都在跳。粉色的转圈、蓝色的跳跃、紫色的旋转，跳累了就在星空下睡着。
- **核心词汇**：unicorn、dance、twirl、leap、spin、stars。
- **核心句型**：`Once upon a time...` / `They danced in the ___ .` / `The ___ unicorn did a ___ .`
- **场景互动**：放音乐，跟着做 twirl、leap、spin 三个动作。
<details>
<summary>🎵 点击展开熏听文本</summary>
Once upon a time, there were unicorns who loved to dance. <br>
They danced in the morning. They danced at night. They danced in the rain. They danced in the sunshine. <br>
The pink unicorn did a twirl. The blue unicorn did a leap. The purple unicorn did a spin. <br>
All the unicorns danced together. They danced and danced until they were tired. <br>
Then they fell asleep under the stars. The end.
</details>

### 🔍 板块四：推理悬疑系列（思维启蒙）

#### 15. The Case of the Missing Snowman（雪人失踪案 · 因果推理）
- **内容简介**：雪在融化，雪人不见了！昨天还在，今天就没了。是谁拿走了？找找线索——帽子、围巾、一摊水……太阳出来了，雪人融化了。谜题解开！
- **核心词汇**：snowman、melting、clues、hat、scarf、puddle、sun。
- **核心句型**：`Where did the snowman go?` / `Here is his ___ .` / `The sun came out. Mystery solved!`

### 📐 正文结构模板
1. **痛点开场**：句型书读腻了、娃只会跟读不会自己说、不肯开口——你是不是也卡在这？
2. **为什么选它**：有情节、有反转、有结尾提问；4 大主题（友谊💛/科普🌍/想象🦄/推理🔍）一套全包；Apple&Orange 角色贯穿，娃看到老朋友停不下来。
3. **怎么读（三阶段）**：① 词汇拓展（指读磨耳朵）→ ② 句型拓展（留白接读+替换造句）→ ③ 生活应用（举 1–2 个你家真实例子最打动人，如「读完烤饼干真去烤了」）。
4. **真实反馈**：放娃跟读、接句、推理「破案」的真实片段，比「完美开口」更引共鸣。
5. **结尾互动**：抛问题引评论，如「你家娃卡在哪个阶段？」「E&F 和 RAZ 怎么搭？评论区聊聊～」
