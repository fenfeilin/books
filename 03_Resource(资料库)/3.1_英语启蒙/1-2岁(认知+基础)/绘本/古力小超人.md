## 书本简介
《古力小超人》是一套非常适合低幼宝宝的英语启蒙绘本。故事围绕古力小超人（Gyuri）和他的朋友们（Leo, Bella, JoJo, Ducky, Tinny）的日常生活与冒险展开。涵盖了四季变化、洗澡、过生日、职业扮演、捉迷藏等丰富的场景。每次朋友们遇到困难，只要大喊“Gyuri! Help us!”，古力小超人就会用各种有趣的方式帮大家解决问题。句型重复率高，非常适合做TPR（全身反应法）互动。

---

## 妈妈心得：绘本备课的三个进阶阶段
- **1️⃣ 第一阶段：词汇拓展期**
  快速过故事，重点在“找东西”。宝宝指哪我说哪，因此需要先把绘本里可能出现的单词查好。
- **2️⃣ 第二阶段：句型拓展期**
  宝宝理解故事后，开始替换造句。我会提前看看有什么可以替换的句子，比如从“I'll hide”到“You'll hide”。
- **3️⃣ 第三阶段：生活应用期**
  脱离绘本，真实场景角色扮演。在游戏中自然输出，学得快记得牢，这就要提前去思考绘本能用到的场景。

## 分册拓展与核心备课内容

### 1. Hello (打招呼与游乐场)
- **内容简介**：朋友们分别做自我介绍并表达爱好（钓鱼、烹饪等）。大家在游乐场玩滑梯卡住了，古力用尾巴把大家拉出来。
- **核心词汇**：fishing, cooking, dancing, playground, slide, cape, tail.
- **核心句型**：`Hello, I'm...` / `I love...` (如 I love sliding!) / `Pull... out!`
- **场景互动**：带宝宝去玩滑梯时使用。联动 Lovevery 《Max and Nana Go to the Park》：*Max counts the steps... 1 2 3. With a little scoot, down he goes.*  
<details>
<summary>🎵 点击展开熏听文本</summary>
Hello! I’m Leo. I love fishing. <br>
Hello! I’m Bella. I love cooking. <br>
Hello! I’m Jojo. I love dancing. <br>
Hello! I’m Teeny. I love singing. <br>
Hello! I’m Ducky. I love reading. <br>
Gyuri! Help us! I’m Gyuri the Superhero! <br>
Pull Jojo out! Pull Bella out! Pull Leo out! Pull everybody out! <br>
We love sliding!
</details>

### 2. Wake up (春天与叫醒)
- **内容简介**：春天到了，小动物们想叫醒冬眠的青蛙、刺猬和蛇，但叫不醒。古力用挠痒痒、摇晃等动作唤醒了大家。
- **核心词汇**：frog, hedgehog, snake, spring.
- **核心句型**：`What’s that noise?` / `...are sleeping.` / `Wake up! It's spring.`
- **场景互动**：早上叫家人起床时用“Wake up, It’s time to get up.” 结合“Wake up bunnies”儿歌播放效果更好。
<details>
<summary>🎵 点击展开熏听文本</summary>
What's that noise? The frogs are sleeping. <br>
Wake up, frogs! It's spring. They don’t wake up. <br>
(同上句型重复 hedgehogs / snakes)<br>
Gyuri! Help us! I’m Gyuri the Superhero! <br>
Wake up. It's spring. Tickle, tap, tap. <br>
Wake up. Wake up. It's spring. Shake, shake, tap, tap.
</details>

### 3. Scrub Scrub Scrub (洗澡与身体部位)
- **内容简介**：认识耳朵、鼻子、肚子等身体部位并擦洗。遇到泡泡危机时，古力来帮忙戳破泡泡并清洁浴室。
- **核心词汇**：ear, nose, belly, foot, scrub, wash, bathtub, bubbles, towel.
- **核心句型**：`Where are your ears? Here they are!` / `Scrub, scrub, scrub!` / `Pop the bubbles!`
- **场景互动**：洗澡时边洗边问部位并发出“Scrub”的动作。联动 Lovevery 洗澡主题：*Take off clothes into hamper -> Splash and play -> Oops, soap in eyes -> Wipe face with washcloth -> Wrap in fluffy towel -> Put on pajamas.* 
<details>
<summary>🎵 点击展开熏听文本</summary>
It's bath time! Let's wash! <br>
Where are your ears? Here they are! Scrub, scrub, scrub! <br>
(重复 nose / feet / belly)<br>
Look at the bubbles! Oh, no! Gyuri! Help us! <br>
I’m Gyuri the Superhero! Pop, pop! Pop the bubbles! <br>
Scrub the tub! Wipe the wall! Wow! Great!
</details>

### 4. Hide and Seek (捉迷藏与地点)
- **内容简介**：大家玩捉迷藏，Leo分别想藏在宠物店（狗叫）、消防站（警报响），最后藏在游乐场管道里卡住了。
- **核心词汇**：hide, seek, pet shop, fire station, playground, stuck, blow.
- **核心句型**：`Let's play hide-and-seek!` / `I'll hide in the...` / `Is Leo in the...?` / `We found you!`
- **场景互动**：在家玩捉迷藏，用纸箱或沙发做掩体：“Are you hiding behind the sofa?”
<details>
<summary>🎵 点击展开熏听文本</summary>
Let's play hide-and-seek! I'll hide. <br>
I'll hide in the pet shop. Oh, no! <br>
I'll hide in the fire station. Oh, no! <br>
I'll hide in the playground. <br>
Is Leo in the pet shop? No! (重复消防站)<br>
Is Leo in the playground? Look in the tube! We found you! <br>
Oh, no! I'm stuck! Gyuri! Help us! <br>
Get in the tube! Blow, blow Leo out! Thank you, Gyuri!
</details>

### 5. Pat Pat Pat (海滩与沙堡)
- **内容简介**：海滩边建沙堡，用铲子挖坑、倒扣桶拍打。大浪冲毁了沙堡，古力骑着冲浪板来建了一个更酷的。
- **核心词汇**：sandcastle, bucket, shovel, cone, wave, surfboard.
- **核心句型**：`Let’s build a sandcastle.` / `Dig a hole.` / `Fill a bucket.` / `Pat! Pat! Pat!`
- **场景互动**：去沙滩或公园沙坑时实操，引导宝宝用英语表达动作（Dig/Scoop/Fill）。
<details>
<summary>🎵 点击展开熏听文本</summary>
I love the beach. Let’s build a sandcastle. <br>
Dig a hole. Scoop! Scoop! Scoop! <br>
Fill a bucket. Pat! Pat! Pat! <br>
Fill a cone. Pat! Pat! Pat! We’re done! <br>
Oh, no! A big wave! Our sandcastle is gone! Our toys are gone. <br>
Gyuri! Help us! I’m Gyuri the Superhero! <br>
Scoop, scoop! Dig a hole. Pat, pat, pat, pat! Fill a cone. <br>
Build a sandcastle. Great! Thanks, Gyuri!
</details>

### 6. Ready go (交通工具与速度)
- **内容简介**：大家骑着摩托、滑板车、汽车比赛。路上遇到乌龟挡路，古力来指挥交通让乌龟通过。
- **核心词汇**：scooter, bike, car, fast, turtle, stop.
- **核心句型**：`I ride my scooter.` / `Let's race!` / `Ready, go!` / `My car goes fast.`
- **场景互动**：户外骑平衡车/滑板车时喊口令“Ready, go!”，或者在家用玩具车比赛，设置路障呼叫古力。
<details>
<summary>🎵 点击展开熏听文本</summary>
I ride my scooter. We ride our bike. I drive my car. <br>
Let's race! Okay! Ready, go! <br>
My scooter goes fast. (重复 bike / car)<br>
Oh, no! Turtles! Gyuri! Help us! <br>
I'm Gyuri the Superhero! <br>
Stop, stop, stop the car/bike/scooter! <br>
Now turtles, go, go, go! Thank you, Gyuri.
</details>

### 7. the birthday party (生日派对与数字)
- **内容简介**：为JoJo办派对，准备了气球、帽子、杯子等（数量都是4）。结果忘了做蛋糕，古力带搅拌机来帮忙。
- **核心词汇**：balloon, hat, cup, fork, cake, mix, light, celebrate.
- **核心句型**：`Let's have a party!` / `What do we need?` / `We need four...` / `Happy birthday!`
- **场景互动**：和宝宝分饰两角办假装派对：“We need four hats. Can you help me find them?”
<details>
<summary>🎵 点击展开熏听文本</summary>
It's Jojo's birthday. Let's have a party! <br>
What do we need? We need balloons. How many? Four. One, two, three, four. <br>
(重复 hats / cups / forks)<br>
We're ready! Oh, no! We need a cake. Gyuri! Help us! <br>
I'm Gyuri the Superhero! Let's bake a cake! <br>
Mix, mix, mix! The cake is ready! <br>
Light the candles! Happy birthday, Jojo!
</details>

### 8. yum yum (秋天采摘与食物)
- **内容简介**：秋天采苹果、坚果、胡萝卜。食物装不下（筐太小），古力骑小摩托带来大篮子并教大家分辨成熟果实。
- **核心词汇**：fall/autumn, pick, basket, apple, nut, carrot, yummy.
- **核心句型**：`Look at the apples.` / `Let's pick...` / `Put them in the basket.` / `They look yummy!`
- **场景互动**：利用家里的水果和玩具篮子玩分类采摘：“Put apples in the red basket.”
<details>
<summary>🎵 点击展开熏听文本</summary>
It's fall. Look at the apples. They look yummy! <br>
Let's pick apples / nuts / carrots. <br>
Pick the apples. Put them in the basket. (重复 nuts / carrots)<br>
Oh, no! Where's the food? It's all gone! <br>
Gyuri! Help us! I'm Gyuri the Superhero! <br>
Pick, pick, pick, pick the apples/nuts/carrots! Let's pick!
</details>

### 9. Go away shadow (夜晚露营与影子)
- **内容简介**：夜晚露营，听到风声猫头鹰叫。看到一个巨大的影子以为是怪物，古力点亮星星，发现是朋友Leo的影子。
- **核心词汇**：dark, night, star, wind, owl, shadow, monster.
- **核心句型**：`Look at the...` / `Feel the wind.` / `Listen to the owl!` / `Go away, shadow/monster!`
- **场景互动**：睡前关灯玩手电筒游戏，观察影子大小变化：“Look at the shadow. It's big/small.”
<details>
<summary>🎵 点击展开熏听文本</summary>
It's dark! It's night! <br>
Look at the stars. Twinkle, twinkle! They're pretty. <br>
Feel the wind. It's cool. Listen to the owl! Hoot, hoot! <br>
Look at the shadow. Oh, no! A monster! Gyuri! Help us! <br>
I'm Gyuri the Superhero! Shine down, big star! <br>
Go, go away, shadow / monster! <br>
Look! It's our friend, Leo!
</details>

### 10. Follow me (冬天足迹与动物)
- **内容简介**：雪地里跟着脚印找动物。发现了兔子、松鼠，最后是一头大熊！大家吓坏了，古力带着大家踮脚、跳跃逃跑。
- **核心词汇**：tracks, bunny, squirrel, bear, follow, jump, tiptoe, run.
- **核心句型**：`Whose tracks?` / `Follow me!` / `It's a...` 
- **场景互动**：在家在地板放几片纸做脚印，模仿书中动作踮脚尖走（tiptoe），躲开睡觉的大熊玩具。
<details>
<summary>🎵 点击展开熏听文本</summary>
Look, tracks! Whose tracks? Follow me! <br>
It's a mommy bunny. <br>
More tracks! Whose tracks? Follow me! It's a squirrel. <br>
More tracks! Whose tracks? Follow me! It's a bear! <br>
Gyuri! Help us! I'm Gyuri the Superhero! <br>
Follow me, follow me! Jump, jump! <br>
Follow me, follow me! Tiptoe, tiptoe! <br>
Follow me, follow me! Run, run and run! Thank you, Gyuri!
</details>

### 11. the rainy day (下雨天与颜色)
- **内容简介**：下雨天大家穿戴红雨衣、黄靴子出门踩水。乌云密布时，古力施法赶走雨和云，迎来了彩虹。
- **核心词汇**：raining, raincoat, boots, umbrella, splash, rainbow, (红黄蓝绿橙等颜色词汇).
- **核心句型**：`It's raining.` / `We can wear our raincoats!` / `I have a [颜色] + [物品]` / `Rain, rain, go away!`
- **场景互动**：下雨天出门前复习：“I will wear my blue raincoat and my yellow boots.”
<details>
<summary>🎵 点击展开熏听文本</summary>
Let's play outside. It's raining. <br>
We can wear our raincoats! I have a red coat. Drip, drop! <br>
I have an orange hat. I have yellow boots. Splash! Splash! <br>
I have a green/blue umbrella. <br>
Oh, no! Black cloud! Gyuri! Help us! <br>
I’m Gyuri the Superhero! <br>
Rain, rain, go away! Cloud, cloud, go away! <br>
Little friends want to play. Go away! Hello! Rainbow!
</details>

### 12. who are you (职业扮演)
- **内容简介**：换装游戏，大家扮舞者、厨师、司机、歌手。JoJo弄乱了衣服不知道扮什么，古力帮他变成了超级英雄！
- **核心词汇**：dress-up, dancer, cook, driver, singer, superhero.
- **核心句型**：`Let's play dress-up!` / `Who are you?` / `I'm a...` / `I like to...`
- **场景互动**：看到街上不同职业的人时引导输出：“I like to help people, so I want to be a doctor.”
<details>
<summary>🎵 点击展开熏听文本</summary>
Let's play dress-up! Okay! <br>
Who are you? I'm a dancer. I like to dance. <br>
(重复 cook/drive/sing 及其对应身份)<br>
Come out, Jojo! Oh, no! Gyuri! Help! <br>
I'm Gyuri the Superhero! <br>
You can be a dancer / cook / driver / singer. <br>
You can be anything. I'm Jojo the Superhero!
</details>

## 阅读轨迹 & 行为观察

- **[[1岁6个月 记录]]**：我们从1岁开始熏听童谣，主要是彩虹兔+巴塔木童谣。看RAZ AA能认出一半水果动物。但**一拿出古力小超人就跑，果断收起来**。
- **[[1岁8个月 记录]]**：突然开窍！能唱出5-6首童谣片段，RAZ AA大部分生活常见名词都知道。我又拿出了《Scrub Scrub Scrub》（洗澡）那本开始，终于能安静听完故事了。
- **[[1岁9个月 记录]]**：**每天必刷古力小超人！** 能自发输出完整句子了，比如：“I‘ll hide”、“Pick xx”、“Help me”、“Here it is”、“Wake up, xxx”。

> **📝 结语总结**：尊重宝宝的节奏很重要！当初抗拒的古力小超人，现在成了最爱。静待花开，每个孩子都有自己的成长时刻表～
