## 📚 分册精读与拓展指南

### 1. What's in your bag
- **内容介绍**：Teeny 和 Ducky、Bella、JOJO、Gyuri 等朋友玩耍时，依次好奇并查看了大家的包：Leon 的包有条纹 T 恤，Ducky 的包装满各类书籍，JOJO 的包装着乐器，Bella 的包有镜子、梳子和发夹，Gyuri 的包全是问题。最后 Teeny 打开自己的小包，里面是彩色气球，朋友们一起吹气球、被气球带飞，安全落地后，故事也随之结束。
- **重点单词**：bag, t-shirt, book(s), guitar(s), drum(s), maraca(s), mirror(s), brush(s), hairpin(s), questions(s), balloon(s)
- **核心句型**：What's in your bag?
- **互动与拓展**：
	- **封面猜一猜**：Why are there so many bags? Whose bags are these？What is inside them?
	- **画面互动**：Which bag is bigger？What color is the balloon? How many balloons？
	- **读后理解**：Who (Teeny, Ducky, Bella, JOJO, Gyuri) / What (Go to party) / How (By balloons, take bags)

### 2. Why is her name monster
- **内容介绍**：Gyuri遇到leo和他的狗monster。Gyuri好奇为什么小狗的名字叫monster。leo说因为他是爱舔人的家伙，他是爱咬靴子的家伙，他是喜欢弄坏桶的家伙。虽然如此，但是leo还是很爱monster，因为leo可以和monster一起看电视，一起跳舞，一起睡觉。
- **重点单词**：monster(s), lick, chew, wreck, watch tv, dance, sleep, face(s), boot(s), bucket(s)
- **核心句型**：What's her name? Her name is ... / Why is her name? / Why do you love..? I can...
- **互动与拓展**：
	- **封面猜一猜**：Whose name is Monster?
	- **画面互动**：What will Leo doing？Fishing (taking fishing rod) -> Leo love fishing / Who is hiding in the grass? Bella, JOJO / What else did they do while watching the tv? Eating snacks.
	- **读后理解**：Who (Leo) / Why is Leo called a puppy monster? (Licking monster, boot monster, bucket monster) / Why does Leo love puppies? (Watch tv, dance, sleep with him)

### 3. Can I have it
- **内容介绍**：Bella、Teeny 和 Gyuri去动物园，看到了长颈鹿，大猩猩，北极熊，鳄鱼，老虎。Gyuri想把他们带回家，但是遭到Bella 和 Teeny的拒绝。正当Gyuri垂头丧气的时候，Bella、Teeny带着Gyuri走到了玩具店，Gyuri开心的抱着“老虎”回家啦。
- **重点单词**：giraffe(s), gorilla(s), alligator(s), tiger(s), polar bear(s)
- **核心句型**：What's that? It's a/an... / Can I have it? / No way. Yes you can.
- **互动与拓展**：
	- **封面猜一猜**：Where are they going? -- Zoo.
	- **画面互动**：The giraffe is too tall, and the house is too short to fit in. / Gorillas can destroy things at home. / The place where polar bears live is too cold. / A crocodile needs to live in a pond, not in a bathtub. / The tiger snores while sleeping and also scratches the wall.
	- **读后理解**：Who (Bella, Teeny, Gyuri) / Where (Zoo) / Why can't Gyuri bring animals home? (The animal need to live in the zoo) / What did Gyuri bring home? (A toy tiger)

### 4. Are we there yet
- **内容介绍**：Teeny 和 Ducky、Bella、JOJO、Gyuri开车去水上乐园，路程非常长，小伙伴非常无聊，玩起说单词游戏，把路上看到的东西都说出来。不知不觉中，就到达了终点~
- **重点单词**：bored, sleepy, cloud(s), truck(s), bicycle(s), tunnel(s), kite(s), dragonfly(dragonflies), water park(s), airplane(s), tree(s)
- **核心句型**：Are we there yet? Not yet/Just wait / What do you see? I see a/an...
- **互动与拓展**：
	- **封面猜一猜**：Where is Ducky driving to？What happens on the way？
	- **画面互动**：What did you see along the way? (Mountain, sun, cloud, truck, car, bakery)
	- **读后理解**：Who (Teeny, Ducky, Bella, JOJO, Gyuri) / What (Drive to water park, play game)

### 5. Whose turn is it
- **内容介绍**：Teeny 和 Ducky、Bella、JOJO、Gyuri 在玩传袋子的游戏，每个小伙伴会从袋子里抽出东西。Gyuri抽出来皇冠，可以扮演国王。Bella抽出白大褂，可以扮演医生。JOJO抽出粉色裙子，可以扮演公主。Teeny抽出了一个大斗篷，大家一起扮演龙。
- **重点单词**：pass, bag(s), round, stop, crown(s), coat(s), dress(es), big, white, pink, king(s), doctor(s), pirate(s), princess(es), dragon(s)
- **核心句型**：Whose turn is it? It's my turn. / What is it? / Do I look like a/an?
- **互动与拓展**：
	- **封面猜一猜**：What's in the bag?
	- **画面互动**：King, Crown, Scepter / Doctor, white coat, stethoscope, syringe / Pirate, boat, one-eyed.
	- **读后理解**：Who (Teeny, Ducky, Bella, JOJO, Gyuri) / What (Role-playing game)

### 6. Did you wash your hands
- **内容介绍**：JOJO上课途中要去洗手间，回来发现手没洗，又重新去洗。洗完回来发现没用肥皂，又重新去洗。用肥皂洗完之后发现没有擦手。重新去擦手之后，发现大家都在洗手。为什么呢？因为要吃午饭了！大家一起开心的吃完饭，然后去洗手洗脸。
- **重点单词**：wash, dry, hand(s), face(s), use, soap, clean, messy, bathroom(s)
- **核心句型**：Did you?
- **互动与拓展**：
	- **封面猜一猜**：When to wash your hands？(Wash hands before eating and after returning home.)
	- **读后理解**：Who (JOJO) / What (Wash hand) / When (After using the restroom and eating) / How (Wet hands, apply soap, scrub for 20 seconds, rinse thoroughly, and dry with a clean towel.)

### 7. What's that sound
- **内容介绍**：听哪里的声音？是leo在吃零食，是jojo在跳绳，是tenny在喝牛奶，是leo在洗澡，是balla在做饭！
- **重点单词**：eat(eating), jump(jumping), take(taking), drink(drinking), make(making), snake(s), milk, rope(s), dinner, bath
- **核心句型**：What's that sound? / sb is doing sth
- **互动与拓展**：
	- **封面猜一猜**：What happen in the room?
	- **画面互动**：There are a lot of snacks on the ground -> Leo is eating / The clothes have been blown away -> JOJO is jumping / Look at the shadow on the ground -> Leo taking bath / In bathroom, in laundary, in bathroom, in kitchen.
	- **读后理解**：Leo eats, JOJO jumps, Leo takes bath, Bella cooks.

### 8. Can I take my ball
- **内容介绍**：Ducky和Gyuri 要出去玩，Gyuri很贪心，想带上球，水枪，捕虫罐，捕蝶网，钓鱼竿，绳子，水桶，铲子，手电筒，相机。Ducky实在没办法，只能拿出小车。Gyuri觉得小车太小，结果Ducky一变，成了一个好大的车。Gyuri推车的时候，终于觉得自己带了太多东西。
- **重点单词**：ball(s), water gun(s), carry, bug jar(s), butterfly net(s), fishing rod(s), rope(s), bucket(s), shovel(s), flashlight(s), camera(s), wagon(s)
- **核心句型**：Can I take my ...? / That's a lot of stuff? / How can you carry it?
- **互动与拓展**：
	- **封面猜一猜**：What is the duck going to do with the basket ? Maybe for a picnic.
	- **画面互动**：Gyuri has many toys. (p1) toy car, badminton racket(羽毛球拍), inflatable wagon(充气小车).
	- **读后理解**：Who (Ducky, Gyuri) / What (Going on a picnic, they have lot of things to bring) / How should they carry them？(A wagon) / Is this wagon big or small? / Do you think Gyuri'll bring this much stuff next time?

### 9. What did you build
- **内容介绍**：大家都在用积木拼东西，Gyuri拼了一个飞机，ducky拼了一个小车，leo拼了一个火车，bennny拼了一个城堡，还有小桥，房子，小船。大家都在小心翼翼的保护自己的作品。然后大家发现，积木全堆成了小山。大家拆了他。
- **重点单词**：toy(s), toy box(es), mountain(s), block(s), airplane(s), car(s), train(s), castle(s), bridge(s), house(s), boat(s), break
- **核心句型**：What time is it? / What did you build? / I build a/an... / Please don't break it.
- **互动与拓展**：
	- **封面猜一猜**：Can you guess what they build with the blocks?
	- **画面互动**：Can I play it？Can I touch it?
	- **读后理解**：Who (JOJO) / What can Jojo break, and what can't Jojo break?

### 10. Is it yummy
- **内容介绍**：bella给大家带来了礼物让大家猜。提示信息有，好吃的，甜的，软的，大的。ducky猜是棉花糖。bella说不是。再给出一个提示，是冷的。大家猜出来了，是冰淇淋。bella打开箱子，结果发现冰淇淋已经化了。
- **重点单词**：guess, yummy, sweet, soft, big, cold, cotton candy, ice cream
- **核心句型**：Can you guess? / It's someting... / Is it ...? Yes, it is.
- **互动与拓展**：
	- **封面猜一猜**：What is in the box?
	- **画面互动**：Pizza -> salty / Cotton candy -> sweet, soft, big / Cake -> sweet, soft, big / Chocolate -> sweet, hard / Cookie -> sweet, hard, small
	- **读后理解**：How many times did you guess? (5) / Did they guess right in the end? (Yes) / Is the food delicious? (They didn't eat, ice cream melted)
